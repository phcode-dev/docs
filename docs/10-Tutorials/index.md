Pick something you want to do and follow the steps. Each guide gives you a small example to work on and shows you how to check the result.

## Start with a small website

[Download the practice website](/tutorials/first-website.zip). It contains a home page, an About page, a Contact page, and a sample picture.

1. **[Add your own photo](/docs/tutorials/add-images).** Open the website and replace the sample picture with one from your computer.
2. **[Link to another page](/docs/tutorials/link-pages).** Make a “Contact me” link that opens the Contact page.
3. **[Share your website](/docs/tutorials/share-website).** Send someone a link to view it or a ZIP they can edit.

You can [look at the practice website](pathname:///tutorials/first-website/index.html) before downloading it.

The guides open with **Phoenix Pro**, using Design Mode for visual editing. Choose **Community (free)** to follow the steps with the code editor and preview side by side. Your choice is remembered as you move between guides. You can complete all three projects with Community.
